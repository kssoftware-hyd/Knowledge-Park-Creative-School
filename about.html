Our Story

A Decade of Inspiring Young Learners

Knowledge Park Creative School was founded in 2014 with a simple but powerful dream — to create a school where every child feels seen, valued and inspired. Starting with Nursery classes and a passionate team, we have grown into one of the most trusted pre-primary English medium schools in the Bhokar area.

  • 2014 – Foundation

    School established in Bhokar with Pre-Primary and Primary sections.

  • 2016 – LKG & UKG Added

    Expanded pre-primary program with dedicated LKG and UKG classes.

  • 2018 – Computer Lab Launched

    Modern computer lab introduced for digital learning from early grades.

  • 2020 – Smart Classrooms

    Installed smart boards and interactive teaching tools in all classrooms.

  • 2022 – Sports Complex

    New sports ground and physical education program introduced.

  • 2024 – 10 Years of Excellence

    Celebrated a decade of nurturing young learners in a safe, creative English medium environment.

  • 2026 – New Academic Year

    Admissions open for 2026–27. New facilities and expanded programs.
