FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for admission?
Birth certificate, Aadhar card of child and parents, previous school TC & mark sheet (if applicable), caste certificate (if applicable), 4 passport size photos of the student.
When does the new academic year start?
The academic year 2026–27 begins in June 2026. We advise applying early as seats are limited and fill up quickly.
Is there a school bus facility available?
Yes, we have a school bus service covering Bhokar and surrounding villages. Please indicate your requirement in the admission form. Transport charges are separate from school fees.
What is the medium of instruction?
We offer Marathi medium and Semi-English medium for Pre-Primary and Primary sections. Secondary sections are in Marathi medium as per the state board curriculum.
Can I visit the school before applying?
Absolutely! We welcome parents to visit our campus. Please call us on 092845 04673 or WhatsApp us to schedule a convenient time for your visit.
Are there any scholarships available?
We encourage meritorious and economically weaker students. Please contact our office directly to discuss available concessions and government scholarship schemes.
