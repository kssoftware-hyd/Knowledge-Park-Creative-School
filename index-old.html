About Us

Building Bright Futures Together

Knowledge Park Creative School is a premier educational institution in Bhokar, Maharashtra, committed to delivering holistic education. We blend academics with arts, sports, and values to develop well-rounded individuals ready to face tomorrow's challenges.

Our dedicated faculty, modern infrastructure, and child-friendly environment make learning an exciting adventure for every student from Nursery to 10th Grade.

Experienced Faculty

Passionate educators with years of expertise dedicated to every child's growth.

Modern Infrastructure

Smart classrooms, computer lab, library and well-equipped science labs.

Safe & Nurturing Environment

Safe campus with CCTV, caring staff and strong parent–teacher communication.

Why Choose Us

Everything a Child Needs to Flourish

We provide a comprehensive learning ecosystem that goes beyond textbooks to nurture creativity, confidence, and character.

Academic Excellence

Structured curriculum following state board norms with regular assessments, remedial classes and 100% board results.

Creative Arts

Drawing, craft, music and dance programs that unleash every child's creative potential and build confidence.

Sports & Activities

Regular sports, yoga and physical education sessions that build teamwork, discipline and healthy habits.

Parent Involvement

Active PTM sessions, parent workshops and open communication channels to keep families connected.

Transport Facility

Safe and reliable school bus service covering Bhokar and surrounding areas for hassle-free commute.

Hygienic Meals

Nutritious and hygienic mid-day meal program ensuring children are energized and focused all day.

Safety & Security

24/7 CCTV surveillance, trained security staff and strict visitor protocols for a fully safe campus.

Awards & Recognition

Consistently recognized for academic and extracurricular achievements at district and state levels.
